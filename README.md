## 🏢 Company Overview

Getso Scraps & Multi-purpose Company Ltd is a Nigerian waste management and recycling company committed to transforming waste into economic opportunities while promoting environmental sustainability.

Our operations focus on:

- Waste Collection
- Scrap Trading
- PET Plastic Recovery
- Recycling Supply Chains
- Circular Economy Solutions
- Climate Action Initiatives

---

## 🌍 GETSO WASTECONNECT

GETSO WASTECONNECT is a climate-tech platform designed to connect:

- Households
- Businesses
- Waste Collectors
- Recyclers
- Logistics Providers
- Manufacturers

through a transparent and efficient recycling ecosystem.
